# Industrial-Internship-Documentation
Industrial Internship at AKIJ Glass Industry Ltd. 2025
# Overview
This repository highlight my internship at AKIJ Glass Industry Ltd. and related report and Book those were submitted by our group. In these documentation, we have presented our learning and experience that we gained from the internship.The internship focused on mainly the manufacturing process of float glass.
# Internship Details
Industry: AKIJ Glass Industry Ltd.
Duration: 1 month ( 01 March 2025 to 31 march 2025)
Group Member: 04
Department: Ceramic and Metallurgical Engineering.
University: Rajshahi University of Engineering and Technology.
# Objectives
-To understand the manufacturing process of Float Glass.
-To gather practical knowledge about industrial operations.
-To know about the raw materials and machineries used in Glass manufacturing.
-To know about quality control and plant operations.
-To relate theoritical knowledge with practical exposure.
# Contents of the Report and Book.
-Introduction of the Glass industry
-Basic Raw materials needs for Glass manufacturing.
-Basic machineries requires for production process.
-Production process of Float Glass.
-Plants related with Glass manufacturing.
-Furnace and production operation.
-Quality control methods.
# Learning Outcomes
-Production process analysis.
-Production observation.
-Technical report and Book writting.
-Designing process Flowchart.
-Data collection in industrial environment.
